# Polyglot Engineering Rules

1. Do not assume Node.js.
2. Do not assume React.
3. Do not assume Next.js.
4. Do not assume npm.
5. Do not assume Docker.
6. Do not assume a database.
7. Do not assume a framework.
8. Determine project requirements first.
9. Inspect deployment constraints before stack selection.
10. Prefer the simplest technology that satisfies the requirements.
11. Lock the selected stack before implementation.
12. Never silently change a locked stack.
13. Keep the project deployable in its actual target environment.
14. Verify claims with evidence.
15. Preserve existing agents and project behavior.
