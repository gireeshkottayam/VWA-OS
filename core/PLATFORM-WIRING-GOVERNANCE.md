# Platform Wiring Governance

A platform is a graph of connected systems. A feature is complete only when its required connections work end-to-end.

## No-Overwrite
Agents must extend and repair before replacing.

## Evidence
No connection is VERIFIED without suitable evidence.

## Change Record
Record what changed, why, affected components, verification, evidence and rollback.

## Secrets
Never expose credentials, tokens or private keys.

## Unknown
Use UNKNOWN or UNVERIFIED when evidence is unavailable.

## Conflicts
When project instructions conflict, stop and surface the conflict instead of silently selecting an architecture.
