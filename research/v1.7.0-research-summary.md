## Conclusion

These signals point to a broader need for **agent reliability, observability, evidence and controlled execution**.

VWA-OS already has development, QA and debugging capabilities. v1.7.0 therefore adds a supervisory Governor rather than another coding specialist.

Community posts are signals, not statistically representative surveys. The Governor must seek independent confirmation and label uncertainty explicitly.
