# Workflow 18 — Platform Wiring Repair

1. Select a reproduced BROKEN/misconfigured edge.
2. Identify upstream/downstream dependencies.
3. Determine the first failing edge.
4. Inspect configuration and contracts.
5. Check environment differences.
6. Form root-cause hypothesis.
7. Define smallest safe repair.
8. Check architecture conflicts.
9. Apply repair.
10. Test repaired edge.
11. Test adjacent critical edges.
12. Verify runtime behavior.
13. Capture evidence.
14. Update wiring map.
15. Record rollback details.

Do not rewrite the platform merely because its wiring is difficult to understand.
