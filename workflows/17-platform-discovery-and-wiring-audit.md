# Workflow 17 — Platform Discovery & Wiring Audit

1. Read project governance and existing agent instructions.
2. Establish project boundaries.
3. Inventory clients and services.
4. Detect languages/frameworks/runtimes.
5. Inventory routes and APIs.
6. Inventory authentication and permissions.
7. Inventory databases/storage.
8. Inventory external integrations.
9. Inventory environment configuration without exposing secrets.
10. Trace critical user journeys.
11. Build PLATFORM-WIRING-MAP.md.
12. Mark critical edges with evidence.
13. Produce wiring risk register.
14. Stop before destructive changes.

No critical connection may be marked VERIFIED without evidence.
